Sarthak Joshi

I am a Lecturer in Economics at the University of Edinburgh.

My research interests are in development and labor economics, with a cross-cutting focus on gender inequality.

My current work examines gendered commuting frictions, labor-market shocks, education policy, and teacher professional development.
